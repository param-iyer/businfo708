Multiple Line Intersections & LP Vertices

Understanding Multi-Intersection Boundaries

When an LP model has 3 active boundaries (Labour, Kiln, and Beans), the feasible region boundary is formed by the lower envelope of all constraints in the positive quadrant.

Feasible Vertices = (0,0) → (0, 140) → Intersection 1 (50, 120) → Intersection 2 (120, 70) → (180, 0)

In this model, compare the two interior choices:

  • Intersection 1 (50, 120): Formed by Labour & Kiln $\rightarrow Z = 6(50) + 10(120) = \mathbf{\$1,500.00}$ (Optimal Vertex!).
  • Intersection 2 (120, 70): Formed by Kiln & Beans $\rightarrow Z = 6(120) + 10(70) = \$1,420.00$.

The Extreme Point Theorem

Because the maximum of a linear objective function occurs at a vertex point, the Simplex algorithm evaluates adjacent vertices to systematically arrive at $(50, 120)$.
